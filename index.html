2. Village BMC Solarization

Installs rooftop solar PV with battery storage at village Bulk Milk Cooling centers, displacing up to 60% of Scope 2 grid power and backup diesel generation.

3. Community Manure Digesters

Builds cooperative bio-gas dome covers over manure lagoons to capture escaping methane for clean cooking fuel and organic bio-fertilizer.
